title: "on losing people and rebuilding"
description: "a personal one. about friendship, faith, and what you build when things fall apart."

---

this one isn't about security.

i lost some close friendships this year. not dramatically — no big fallout, no single moment. just a slow drift that eventually became distance that became absence.

it hit harder than i expected. these were people i thought would be around for a long time.

---

what i've noticed: when the scaffolding around you falls away, you find out pretty quickly what you're actually standing on.

for me, that foundation is faith. i don't talk about it much publicly but it's the most real thing in my life. the anchor when everything else shifts.

the rebuilding hasn't been fast. it's been quiet — more time in the diary, more time in prayer, more time just being honest with myself about what i want and why.

what came out of it: a lot of clarity. about the kind of person i want to be, the kind of work i want to do, the kind of relationships i actually want to invest in.

---

i'm sharing this because this site is meant to be honest. the grind posts are easy to write — here's what i did, here's what i learned. the personal stuff is harder.

but the grind doesn't happen in a vacuum. the person doing the work matters too.

if you're going through something similar — the slow loss of people you thought would stay — it gets cleaner. not necessarily easier, but cleaner.

you find out what you're actually made of. that part is worth it.
